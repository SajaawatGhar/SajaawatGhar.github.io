Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This privacy notice describes the current Sajaawat Ghar editorial site and its public contact pathway. It should be reviewed if new accounts, checkout tools, analytics, or other services are introduced.

1. Current contact pathway

The site offers a link to Sajaawat Ghar's official Instagram account. If you choose to contact us through Instagram, your message is handled on that platform under its applicable terms and privacy practices. Please avoid sending payment card numbers or other sensitive information in an initial enquiry.

2. Website browsing

The website loads product data, images, fonts, and application resources needed to display the pages. Hosting providers and external services may process technical connection information, such as device/browser details and network logs, according to their configurations and independent privacy notices.

3. Local preferences

Some browser features may remember preferences such as the selected language or product-filter state. Such preferences can usually be cleared using your browser's site-data controls.

4. Enquiry information

If you choose to send an enquiry through Instagram, you may voluntarily provide a name, contact information, a screenshot, a product name, a city, or details about a room. Share only what is needed for your enquiry. Actual orders, payments, and courier delivery processing are not currently enabled through this site.

5. External content

Photos shown as styling references may be based on licensed third-party imagery. External social, font, or infrastructure services may process browsing requests under their own privacy policies. Their practices are outside the direct control of this site.

6. Security and retention

No website or online communications platform can guarantee complete security. Once customer operations are introduced, retention, deletion requests, security practices, and any use of third-party processors should be documented in an updated notice based on actual technical and business practices.

7. Your questions

For a question about the site or a message you sent to the brand, contact @sajaawat_ghar on Instagram. Third-party data-rights requests relating to Instagram should also be handled through that platform's procedures.

8. Changes

This notice may change when the site begins handling real orders, customer accounts, newsletters, reviews, or additional analytics. The effective date will be updated when material changes are made.

This is a preview notice and should be checked against the final hosting, analytics, and customer data arrangements before launch.
